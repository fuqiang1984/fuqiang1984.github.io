Curious Jason · English vocabulary

One word. A clear meaning. A spoken example.

YouTube Vocabulary Automation is a personal publishing tool operated by Qiang Fu for the Curious Jason YouTube channel. It turns prepared vocabulary scripts into numbered vertical videos with British English pronunciation, a meaning and an example sentence.

How it works

  1. The channel operator selects a prepared batch in GitHub Actions.
  2. The workflow generates the videos and makes them available to download.
  3. If uploading is selected, it uses the operator’s Google authorization to upload to the configured channel with the selected visibility.

Why Google access is requested

The app requests permission to upload YouTube videos and read YouTube account information. The read permission is used to verify the connected channel’s ID before uploading. It does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive or your Google password.

This is not a public account-registration service. Use is currently limited to the operator’s own channel. Google and YouTube may impose quota, verification and publishing restrictions.
