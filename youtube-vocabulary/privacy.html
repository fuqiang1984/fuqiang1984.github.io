Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 September 2026

This policy covers YouTube Vocabulary Automation, operated by Qiang Fu for the Curious Jason channel, and these app information pages.

Information accessed and used

  • Google authorization: OAuth client credentials and access and refresh tokens allow the selected workflow to act on the authorized YouTube channel. The app does not receive your Google password.
  • Channel information: the app requests YouTube read-only permission and currently uses it to check the authorized channel ID against the configured channel.
  • Video content: prepared words, pronunciations, definitions, examples, generated audio and video, titles, descriptions and visibility settings are processed to make and upload Shorts.
  • Upload records: word numbers, upload status, video IDs or URLs and returned visibility are recorded to track progress and avoid duplicate uploads.
  • Support: if you email the operator, your address and the information you provide are used to handle your request.

Where information goes

Google and YouTube receive authorization requests and uploaded videos and metadata. GitHub hosts the workflow, configured repository secrets, generated artifacts and upload records. Vocabulary text is sent to Google Translate’s speech service through gTTS to generate narration; OAuth credentials and channel information are not sent to that speech service.

The app does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models. Access is limited to operating and supporting the authorized publishing workflow, security and applicable legal obligations. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

The local authorization helper writes a token file on the operator’s computer. The operator copies it into a GitHub Actions secret. GitHub encrypts repository secrets; the workflow makes credentials available to the upload step and does not intentionally print them or include them in artifacts. The local credential files remain until the operator deletes them. Repository and device access must be restricted by the operator; no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Retention

The current main workflow sets generated artifact retention to 14 days, and the sample validation workflow to 7 days. Downloaded copies remain on the operator’s device until deleted. Upload records are committed to the private automation repository and remain, including in Git history, until removed by the operator. GitHub also retains workflow logs under the repository’s retention settings. Authorization secrets remain until removed or replaced; Google may expire or revoke tokens. Uploaded videos remain on YouTube until deleted there.

Revoke access or request deletion

You can revoke the app’s Google access at any time using Google Account permissions. To stop the workflow, also disable it and remove its GitHub secret. Revocation stops future authorized API access but does not automatically erase existing files, repository history or uploaded videos.

For deletion of app-held data, email john.fu1984@gmail.com with your channel identifier and the request. Do not send passwords or tokens. The operator will verify the request and remove applicable app-held data from controlled storage, including relevant artifacts and repository history where necessary. Copies outside the operator’s control are subject to their provider’s retention rules. Deleting app-held records does not delete videos on YouTube; manage those separately in YouTube Studio.

This website and third parties

These app pages add no analytics scripts, advertising or tracking cookies. GitHub Pages may process IP addresses and request information to provide hosting. Google, YouTube and GitHub process information under their own policies: Google Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement. The app uses YouTube API Services.

Changes and contact
